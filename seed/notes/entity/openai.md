# OpenAI

**OpenAI** is an AI research organization and company known for pioneering generative pre-trained transformers (GPTs) and frontier LLMs.

---

## Key Models & Innovations

- **GPT Series**: GPT-1 (2018), GPT-2 (2019), GPT-3 (2020), GPT-4 (2023), GPT-4o (2024), o1 / o3 reasoning series.
- **ChatGPT**: Popular consumer application accelerating generative AI adoption worldwide.
- **Embeddings & Speech**: Text-embedding-3, Whisper (speech recognition), Sora (video generation).

---

## Technical Contributions to Wiki Concepts
