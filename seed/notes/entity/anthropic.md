# Anthropic

**Anthropic** is an AI safety and research company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers. Anthropic develops the **Claude** family of frontier AI models.

---

## Key Innovations & Contributions

- **Claude Model Family**: Claude 3 (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus), Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7. Known for high reasoning capabilities, code generation, and 200k+ token context windows.
- **Constitutional AI**: Alignment methodology using AI-written critiques and principles rather than pure human preference feedback.
- **Agentic Best Practices**: Published authoritative guides on [[Building Effective Agents]] emphasizing simple, composable code over complex orchestration frameworks.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: Open standard for connecting AI models to local filesystems, tools, and external services.
