# Meta AI

**Meta AI** (FAIR - Fundamental AI Research) is the artificial intelligence research organization at Meta Platforms.

---

## Key Contributions

- **LLaMA Series (Open Weights)**: LLaMA 1, LLaMA 2, LLaMA 3, LLaMA 3.3. Transformed the open-source LLM landscape by releasing competitive weights for public research and commercial use.
- **PyTorch**: Deep learning framework created by Meta, powering nearly all academic and commercial LLM training and inference.
- **Segment Anything (SAM) & CodeLlama**: Specialized vision and code generation foundation models.
